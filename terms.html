rules for every license

  • credit is required wherever credits are listed: "prod. JJ Brunet" plus any co-producers named on the beat.
  • leases are non-exclusive. the same beat can be leased to other artists until it's sold exclusively. if a beat is sold exclusively, existing leases stay valid under their terms.
  • you may not resell, re-license, or give away the beat itself (with or without vocals over it as a "beat"), or claim you produced it.
  • you may not register the beat or a song using it with content id or similar systems in a way that claims the instrumental itself.
  • when you reach a stream or video limit, upgrade to a higher license before going over it.
  • some beats use licensed samples or co-producer material with extra terms. those are stated on the beat's page and in your purchase (for example, "long overdue" keeps VGS Midnight's producer tag in every file).
  • licenses are personal to the buyer and can't be transferred.
