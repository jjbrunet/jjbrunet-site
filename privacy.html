privacy policy

last updated november 15, 2026

short version: the only personal info this site asks for is your email, and only when you download a free beat. it's never sold.

what we collect

  • email + the beat you downloaded, when you use a free download. it's sent to us through FormSubmit, which forwards it to our email inbox.
  • purchase info, if you buy a lease. checkout happens on Payhip and payments are processed by Stripe. we get your name, email and what you bought. we never see your card details.
  • messages you send us by email or instagram.

what we use it for

  • delivering your files and licenses
  • letting you know when new beats drop (reply "stop" to any email and we'll stop)
  • answering your questions

we don't sell, rent or trade your info.

other services this site uses

  • Cloudflare (hosting): may log basic technical data like ip address for security.
  • Google Fonts: your browser loads fonts from Google, which receives your ip address.
  • FormSubmit, Payhip, Stripe: as described above, each under their own privacy policies.
  • links to Instagram and YouTube: those sites have their own policies once you visit them.

cookies

this site doesn't set cookies or use analytics or ad tracking. Payhip and Stripe may use cookies on their checkout pages.

your choices

you can ask us to see, correct or delete your info at any time by emailing us. we keep download emails only as long as we're sending new-beat updates.

kids

this site isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect info from them.
